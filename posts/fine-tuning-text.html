Fine-tuning a small transformer for text classification

Week 14

I fine-tuned DistilBERT to classify short support-style messages. A small model was enough, and it trained on a single modest GPU.

What mattered

  • A TF-IDF plus logistic regression baseline, which scored baseline F1. The transformer reached transformer F1.
  • Per-class F1, not just overall accuracy, since classes were imbalanced.
  • Reading about fifty wrong predictions by hand. Many were genuinely ambiguous labels.

Takeaway

The gain over the simple baseline was real but not huge. For a production system I would weigh that gain against inference cost and latency.
