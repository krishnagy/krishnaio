First real model: the baseline came first

Week 3

For my first tabular project I picked a customer churn dataset. Before any tuning I trained the simplest model I could defend: logistic regression on a few cleaned features.

What the baseline gave me

It scored your baseline metric on the held-out set. Every later model had to beat that number, which kept me from celebrating improvements that were not real.

Then gradient boosting

XGBoost improved the score to your boosted metric. The gain was real but smaller than I expected, and feature engineering moved the result more than hyperparameter search did.

Lesson

Accuracy was a poor choice because only a minority of customers churned. I switched to precision, recall, and PR-AUC, and wrote down why in the project README.
