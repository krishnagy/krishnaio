Starting point: what I knew and what I didn't

Week 1

I can program and I can learn systems quickly. What I could not do was explain why a model works, so I wrote that down as the gap to close.

What I decided

  • Refresh the maths that shows up constantly: linear algebra, probability, and gradients. Not a full degree, just enough to read a loss curve and know what it means.
  • Get fluent with NumPy and pandas before touching a framework.
  • Build something end to end every few weeks rather than only finish courses.

First exercise

I implemented linear regression with gradient descent in plain NumPy and compared it with scikit-learn. The coefficients matched to N decimal places. That small check made the library feel less like magic.
